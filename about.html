OUR MISSION

STORIES BUILT WITH PURPOSE

Biggs Media Studio Publishing is focused on creating original novels, graphic novels, characters, and fictional universes with their own identities, histories, conflicts, and possibilities.

The mission is simple: build bold stories from the ground up and create worlds that can continue growing across multiple books, characters, and generations of storytelling.

THE VISION

WORLDS BEYOND THE ORDINARY

From grounded stories to superheroes, science fiction, cosmic adventures, tactical characters, androids, warriors, villains, and larger interconnected universes, Biggs Media Studio is designed to explore many different kinds of storytelling.

At the center of that vision is the BMS Universe — an expanding collection of original characters and stories with room to grow into something much larger.

MEET THE FOUNDER

JOSE VASQUEZ

Jose Vasquez is the founder and creator behind Biggs Media Studio Publishing. His creative vision centers on developing original characters, building connected fictional worlds, and turning ideas into finished stories.

Biggs Media Studio was created as an independent home for those stories — a place where new concepts can grow into novels, graphic novels, characters, and long-term creative properties.

THE FUTURE OF BMS

THE STORY IS JUST BEGINNING

Biggs Media Studio continues to develop new books, graphic novels, characters, and worlds. As the catalog expands, so will the universe behind it.
